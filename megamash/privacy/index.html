Mega Mash Privacy Policy

Spotlessness, LLC · Last updated October 4, 2026

This policy covers the game Mega Mash (also listed as "Mega Mash: City Stomp" and "Mega Mash: Monster Smash") for iPhone and Android.

Information we collect

None. Mega Mash does not collect, store, or send any personal information. There is no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The game works completely offline.

Information saved on your device

Your coins, high score, unlocked monsters, and music setting are saved only on your own device so the game remembers your progress. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your name, payment card, or billing details. Those companies handle your payment information under their own privacy policies.

Children

Mega Mash does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
